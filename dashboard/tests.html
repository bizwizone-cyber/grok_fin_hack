What the red team does

Grok (x-ai/grok-4.7 via OpenRouter) writes new wordings of the sentences the system reads from free text — KYC-team notes, ages and deaths in hit profiles, places a customer lived — using invented people only. Each wording runs through the real reader functions of the selected version. A WRONG reading is a wrong fact (it can clear a real match); missed means no fact (it costs a review). system/run itself calls no model.

The changed decisions were checked by hand against the source records. These are our output rows, already published in output/dev.jsonl.

The tests

  • Practice labels (50 hits). The answered dev hits. Scored exactly like the official validate.py --score: decision score, unsafe clearances, reason codes, customer records, plus whether the evidence equals the reference evidence.
  • Full dev run (819 hits). Decision mix, run time, and two runs compared byte for byte (10% of the score is the same answers across five runs).
  • Grok red team. tools/grok_redteam.py: new wordings from Grok and a hand-written set, graded ok / missed / WRONG. Suggestions Grok makes are approved by a person before they reach system/run.
  • Synthetic benchmark (10,000 hits). Ten scenarios of 1,000 hits from generate_synthetic_bench.py. Its answers are our team's reading of the SOP, not the organisers'; its evidence uses a different convention, so evidence is not compared there.
Rebuild after any change: tools/build_test_dashboard.py (takes about two minutes; needs the dev data locally).
